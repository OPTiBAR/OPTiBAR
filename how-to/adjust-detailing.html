Adjust the detailing of a project#

The Special Lengths and Strips pages of the settings change the detailing beyond the Basic page. Both changes below are optional.

Change the development, overlap and bend lengths#

OPTiBAR calculates these lengths from the concrete and steel of the SAFE model when the file is loaded; see Special lengths. Change them when the conditions of the foundation call for it, for example closely spaced bars or shear rebars.

  1. On the Special Lengths page, find the row of the bar diameter.

  2. Edit the value, in cm. With Automatic checked, edit only the bottom development length (Ld); the top Ld and the overlaps follow from it.

  3. Press Calculate again.

To go back to the calculated values, press Reset.

Set the typical rebars of a strip#

By default, every strip gets typical rebars at the interval of the Basic page. To give one strip a different number of bars or a different diameter:

  1. On the Strips page, select the strip and the level (top or bottom).

  2. Enter the Number of bars and the Diameter.

  3. Press Set.

For example, a strip 1.2 m wide gets 6 typical bars with an interval of 20 cm; set its top level to 8 to place 8 bars instead. To remove the override, select the strip and level and press Erase.

OPTiBAR places the bars you set even when they are less than the minimum steel, and reports a warning, see Typical rebars.
