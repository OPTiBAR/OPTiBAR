Settings#

The settings on the left of the window, in the Basic, Special Lengths and Strips pages. Hover the ⓘ next to a setting in the app for a short description. View > Dark Theme switches the app to a dark theme.

Type of Request#

Value

Meaning

Computation

Details the foundation once, with the settings below.

Analysis

Repeats the detailing for a range of Total and Stack, and compares the rebar mass of each combination.

Special Lengths and Strips#

Setting

Meaning

Special Lengths

The development length (Ld, top and bottom), overlap length (top and bottom) and bend lengths (90° and 135°) of each bar diameter, in cm. Calculated from the materials of the SAFE model when the file is loaded, see Special lengths.

Special Lengths, Automatic

When checked, the top Ld and the overlaps follow from the bottom Ld.

Special Lengths, Reset

Restores the calculated values.

Strips

The number and diameter of the typical rebars of single strips and levels, instead of the interval. Set applies them, Erase removes them.
